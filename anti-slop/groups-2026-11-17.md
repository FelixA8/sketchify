Scope: Group/Ungroup actions, selection behavior, nested member editing, and persisted group relationships.

- Source and content PASS: the implementation lives in TypeScript and React. Import normalizes group IDs; grouping reuses the existing scene representation and persistence. No dependencies, assets, marketing claims, or unrelated UI changes were added.
- Purpose PASS: two text actions share the existing object menu and shortcut hints to make grouping available beside other selection actions. The existing purple outline, corner handles, and rotation handle show the whole selection. New commands appear in the shortcut help. DESIGN.md records these decisions.
- Direction and liveliness PASS: retained ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 1 / MOTION 1, the canvas focal point, compact floating controls, system type, and purple selection accent. The change adds no animation, decoration, colors, or panel layout.
- Functional controls PASS: Group creates a group and disables for fewer than two independent components, a single existing group, or locked selections. Ungroup removes one selected level and disables for selections without a group or with locked members. React/JSDOM clicks verify each action in both themes at desktop and mobile logical widths; both close the menu and restore canvas focus. Cmd/Ctrl shortcuts, Shift-click, marquee selection, double-click, Enter, and Escape have regression coverage. No handler errors were recorded.

The 22 grouping cases cover transforms, scopes, clipboard relationship remapping, nested ungrouping, locked groups, labels, frames, undo/redo, reload, and editable export. Existing renderer and legacy compatibility cases pass.
